What changed in each version of Scrap Mechanic Sync. Newest first.
The tool shows the entries you have not seen yet after it updates itself.
Format: a version number alone on a line, then one "- " line per change.

0.2.6
- The Play and Join screens now say what to do if Steam asks to "Launch Game with custom arguments": click Continue.

0.2.5
- When your friend has the world and is probably playing, the tool says so. Press J and the game starts and joins them, with no need to find them in Steam.

0.2.4
- The tool stops and asks before uploading a world that is much smaller than the shared one, in case a new world was made with the same name.
- It warns you when your friend's save was made with a newer version of the game than yours.
- It tells you when the game is running without the world being checked out to you.
- New: press A for Activity, to see who played when and the messages they left.
- New: press D for Diagnostics. It checks the setup and copies a report you can paste to your friend.

0.2.3
- The game now opens straight into the shared world, skipping the main menu.
- The tool checks for updates while the menu is open, not only when you start it.
- After an update, this screen tells you what changed.

0.2.2
- The window opens taller, and the menu shrinks to fit a small window, so the top is never cut off.

0.2.1
- Move through the menu with the up and down arrow keys and press Enter.
- Options that cannot be used right now are greyed out, with the reason beside them.
- The status box refreshes by itself every 30 seconds.
- After a session you see how long you played.
- Dates read "today" and "yesterday".
- Restore a backup shows the message left with each online version.

0.2.0
- Leave a message about your save for the next player.
- "Wait for world to be uploaded" notifies you when your friend has finished.
- Closing the window while you play no longer stops the upload.
- Uploads and downloads are about three times smaller.
- A desktop shortcut for starting the game through this tool.
- Two players pressing Play at the same moment can no longer both get the world.
