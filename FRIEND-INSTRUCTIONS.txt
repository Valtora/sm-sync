SCRAP MECHANIC SYNC - HOW TO USE

SETUP (once)

1. Right-click the zip file, choose "Extract All", and extract it somewhere you will
   find it again, for example your Documents folder. Keep all the files together.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Scrap Mechanic Sync".
   If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click "More info" and then
   "Run anyway". If it shows "Open File - Security Warning", click "Run".
3. A black and orange window opens and asks for your name. Type it and press Enter.
   Use a different name from your friend.
4. It asks whether to put a shortcut on your desktop. Press Y.
   From now on, ALWAYS start the game with that shortcut, never from Steam.


EVERY TIME YOU WANT TO PLAY

1. Make sure Scrap Mechanic is closed.
2. Double-click the "Scrap Mechanic (shared world)" shortcut on your desktop.
3. Look at the box at the top. The "World" line says who has the world.
4. If it says AVAILABLE (or CHECKED OUT BY YOU), press 1.
5. The game starts by itself. LEAVE THE WINDOW OPEN while you play.
6. The game opens straight into the shared world. If it shows the main menu instead,
   load the world yourself as normal.
7. When you are done, quit the game completely (back to the desktop).
8. Wait for the green line that says:
       "The world is uploaded and available for the next player."
9. It then asks for a message for your friend, for example what you built. Type one and
   press Enter, or just press Enter to skip. Now you can close the window.

If your friend has the world and you want to know when they are done, press W and leave
the window open. Windows shows a notification when the world is available.

PLAYING TOGETHER AT THE SAME TIME

Only ONE of you uses this tool: that person presses 1 and hosts. The other starts Scrap
Mechanic from Steam as normal and joins through the friends list.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

"CHECKED OUT BY <name>"
   Your friend has the newest copy of the world and has not uploaded it yet. They are
   playing, or forgot to finish. Message them. Do not play until it says AVAILABLE,
   or your progress will be lost.

Steam asks about a "Cloud conflict"
   Choose the LOCAL files.

You closed the window by accident while playing
   Nothing to do. The upload still happens when you quit the game, and Windows shows
   a notification saying how it went.

The upload failed
   Open the tool again and press 2.

Red text
   It always says what happened and what to do. If you are not sure, go back to the
   menu, press D, wait for it to finish, then paste (Ctrl+V) into a message to your
   friend. Do not play until it is sorted.

Press H in the menu for a longer explanation of how it all works.

The tool updates itself. If it says it has updated, that is normal.
