SCRAP MECHANIC SYNC - HOW TO USE

SETUP (once)

Your friend sent you a message with a download link and a long invite code that starts
with SMSYNC1. Keep that message private: the code is the key to your shared world.

1. Download the zip from the link in the message. Right-click it, choose "Extract All",
   and extract it somewhere you will find it again, for example your Documents folder.
   Keep all the files together.
2. Go back to your friend's message and copy the whole of it (select it, Ctrl+C).
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Scrap Mechanic Sync".
   If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click "More info" and then
   "Run anyway". If it shows "Open File - Security Warning", click "Run".
4. A black and orange window opens. Press 1 for "I have an invite code". It reads the
   code from what you copied. If it asks for the code, paste with Ctrl+V and press Enter.
   If the message lists mods from the Steam Workshop, open each link, press Subscribe,
   and let Steam download it before you play.
5. It asks for your name. Type it and press Enter. Use a different name from your friend.
6. It asks whether to put a "Scrap Mechanic - Sync" shortcut on your desktop. Press Y.
   From now on, ALWAYS start the game with that shortcut, never from Steam.

If you are the one starting a shared world for your group, press 2 on that first screen
instead and follow the steps it shows. Afterwards press F to invite your friends.

Esc always goes back a step, so you cannot get stuck on a setup screen.


EVERY TIME YOU WANT TO PLAY

1. Make sure Scrap Mechanic is closed.
2. Double-click the "Scrap Mechanic - Sync" shortcut on your desktop.
3. Look at the box at the top. The "World" line says who has the world.
4. If it says AVAILABLE (or CHECKED OUT BY YOU), press 1.
5. The game starts by itself. LEAVE THE WINDOW OPEN while you play.
6. The game opens straight into the shared world. If it shows the main menu instead,
   load the world yourself as normal.
7. When you are done, quit the game completely (back to the desktop).
8. Wait for the green line that says:
       "The world is uploaded and available for the next player."
9. It then asks for a message for your friend, for example what you built. Type one and
   press Enter, or just press Enter to skip. Now you can close the window.

If your friend has the world and you want to know when they are done, press W and leave
the window open. Windows shows a notification when the world is available.

If you share more than one world, the tool starts on a list of them. Press the number
of the world you want. Press M in the menu to get back to that list.

PLAYING TOGETHER AT THE SAME TIME

One of you presses 1 and hosts. The other opens the tool, sees CHECKED OUT BY the host,
and presses J. The game starts and joins the host by itself. The host must already be
inside the world. If you land on the main menu instead, press Shift+Tab in the game, find
your friend in the list and choose Join Game.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

"CHECKED OUT BY <name>"
   Your friend has the newest copy of the world and has not uploaded it yet.
   If they are playing right now, press J to join their game.
   If you want to play on your own, wait until it says AVAILABLE. Do not load the
   world yourself before then, or your progress will be lost.

Steam asks to "Launch Game with custom arguments"
   Click Continue. That is this tool telling the game which world to open or which
   friend to join.

Steam shows "Unable to sync", or joining says "Connection lost"
   Steam has lost its own connection. Quit the game, exit Steam completely (Steam menu,
   Exit), start Steam again, and try once more. Your world is not affected.

Steam asks about a "Cloud conflict"
   Choose the LOCAL files.

You closed the window by accident while playing
   Nothing to do. The upload still happens when you quit the game, and Windows shows
   a notification saying how it went.

The upload failed
   Open the tool again and press 2.

Red text
   It always says what happened and what to do. If you are not sure, go back to the
   menu, press D, wait for it to finish, then paste (Ctrl+V) into a message to your
   friend. Do not play until it is sorted. Names, keys and Steam IDs are taken out of
   that report for you.

You think the tool itself is at fault
   Press I in the menu. It opens a page on GitHub with the report filled in, where you
   can describe what happened. Nothing is sent until you press the button on that page.

Press H in the menu for a longer explanation of how it all works.

The tool updates itself. If it says it has updated, that is normal.
